About Advanced Video Compressor
-------------------------------

Advanced Video Compressor for Windows, from WinSoftMagic, Inc. , is designed to compress digital video files by using the most advanced video compression algorithms and technology.

Advanced Video Compressor is an excellent way to convert video for portable devices, prepare video files for sharing or storing, for private or business use: 

*        Reduce the size of your video files 

Advanced Video Compressor can reduce file size impressively - without any loss of quality. Smaller files take less space and can be uploaded and downloaded more quickly: effectively optimize videos for faster downloading from the Web, faster e-mail smaller video files - avoiding exceeding size limits. 

*        Convert video files for portable devices 

Advanced Video Compressor can easily convert your video files for using them on portable devices, such as a mobile phone or a media player. 

*        Convert videos to MPEG4, H.264  from other formats 

Advanced Video Compressor can help you to convert video files from many formats to the most  modern ones by using a selection of our advanced video/audio codecs. 

You can easily process multiple videos just in several clicks, as well as a single video.

Digital video camera users benefit most from Advanced Video Compressor's excellent abilities. It can be the perfect add-on to your other video software.

INSTALLING Advanced Video Compressor
------------------------------------

Requirements: 
------------
Windows 7, Vista, XP, ... Windows 95.
When installed it occupies about 12 Mbytes of disk space.  

Installing:
----------
The installation is quite simple - unzip avideocompr.zip, just run 
the avideocompr.exe and follow the directions given in the screen. 
The automatic installation procedure will copy all the necessary 
files to the specified directory and create a shortcut for the 
program. After installing, AVC can be launched from the "Start" 
menu.

Upgrading:
---------
To upgrade your version to the latest version of AVC, just install 
the latest version to the folder, which contains a previous version 
of AVC.

Uninstalling:
------------
You can uninstall the program from the Control Panel  Add/Remove 
Applications window.  Open the Control Panel from the "Start" menu,
double click Add/Remove Programs, and double click on the "Advanced
Video Compressor" entry in the dialog box.
